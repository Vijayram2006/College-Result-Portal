# Student guide

**Portal:** https://kvell-result-portal.vercel.app — works on any phone or computer.

1. Choose **Student**.
2. Type your **Register Number** and your **Date of Birth** as on college records.
3. Press **Sign in**. If a picture with characters appears (after wrong attempts), type the characters you see.

**What you see:** your profile, published results per semester and assessment (IAT-1, IAT-2, End Semester), grade
points, SGPA/CGPA, internal marks once the COE releases them, and your registered subjects. You can download your
grade sheet as a PDF. Results online are **provisional**; the marksheet from the Controller of Examinations is the
official record.

**Privacy:** only you can see your results. Do not share your date of birth with others, and sign out on shared
computers. See the [privacy policy](https://kvell-result-portal.vercel.app/privacy.html).

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Invalid register number or date of birth" | Check both; the date can be typed as 14-03-2007 or picked from the calendar. |
| "Too many failed attempts" | Wait 15 minutes. |
| "The student result portal is closed" | Results are not open yet; try later. |
| "Starting the result server…" | Wait up to a minute. |
| A mark looks wrong | Apply for revaluation in the portal when the window is open, or contact your department. |
