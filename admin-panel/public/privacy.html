Privacy Policy

Draft for approval by the university. Last updated 17 October 2026.

Who we are

The Result Portal and the COE Panel are run by the Office of the Controller of Examinations (COE) of KVELL University (Karpaga Vinayaga group of colleges) to publish examination results and manage marks. The COE is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect, and why

  • Students: register number, name, date of birth, department, year, semester, admission category, marks, grades and results. Used only to publish results to the student, the department and the COE, and to issue marksheets.
  • Staff (COE, HOD, faculty): username, name, optional email, phone and designation, and records of what each account does in the system (the audit log). Used to run the examination process and keep it accountable.
  • Technical: the network address of each sign-in and change (kept in the audit log), and anonymous error reports from the browser (no names or marks).

We do not collect anything else, we do not use the data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties except the hosting providers that store it for us.

Who can see it

  • A student sees only their own results.
  • An HOD sees only their own department; faculty see only the subjects and students of their department.
  • Each college COE sees only their own college; the Chief COE may view all colleges.

How it is protected

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. COE accounts use two-step sign-in. Every change by staff is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited. Access is checked on the server for every request.

Where it is stored and for how long

The data is stored with the hosting providers named in the university's hosting agreement. Results and marks are kept for as long as the university must keep academic records. Audit entries are kept for 7 years and notifications for one year (see the retention policy).

Your rights

You may ask the COE office to see the data held about you, to correct anything that is wrong, or to raise a grievance. Corrections to marks follow the university's revaluation rules. Contact the Office of the Controller of Examinations.
