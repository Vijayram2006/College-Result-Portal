Terms of Use

Draft for approval by the university. Last updated 17 October 2026.

Results are provisional

Results shown on this portal are provisional and for information only. The marksheet issued and signed by the Controller of Examinations is the official record. If the portal and the marksheet differ, the marksheet is correct. The COE may withdraw and correct a published result.

Your account

  • Use only your own register number or staff account. Do not share passwords or two-step codes.
  • Staff are responsible for everything done with their account; every change is recorded.
  • Trying to see other people's results, guessing dates of birth or passwords, or overloading the service is misuse and may be reported to the university.

Availability

The portal may be unavailable during maintenance or result publication. The current state is shown on the system status page.

Contact

Questions about results or these terms: Office of the Controller of Examinations, KVELL University.
